My Story

Building Human-Centered AI for Real-World Problems

When I moved to campus, I experienced firsthand the chaos of finding a safe hostel room, deciphering unwritten mess policies, and waiting days for simple maintenance requests. I realized that modern conversational AI could bridge this communication barrier and give every student a reliable, friendly copilot.

That insight sparked Hostel Buddy AI. Today, I dedicate my engineering focus to creating tactile, gorgeous, and delightfully intuitive software powered by generative AI.
